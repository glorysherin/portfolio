CampEagle

Camp Eagle Navigator is a camp management app that keeps campers' families and staff connected during the camp season. It puts schedules, messages, notifications and photos in one place, so families don't rely on scattered emails and shared drives.

Key features

  • Daily schedule and home feed of camp updates
  • Interactive campus map with points of interest
  • Real-time messaging with offline support
  • Push notifications that open the right account and screen
  • Photo viewing and saving to the device
  • React Native
  • FCM
  • FastAPI
  • Docker
  • AWS S3 + CloudFront
